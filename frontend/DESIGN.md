# CYPHER conversation studio

The product feels like a place to rehearse a real conversation. Photography sets the situation; the conversation itself stays readable and focused.

## Foundations

- Manrope for headings; DM Sans for controls and reading. Variable fonts are hosted with the app.
- Charcoal surfaces with a coral accent, carried through practice, authentication, reports, and progress. Light appearance uses the same semantic tokens.
- Cards use 20-24px corners, controls 10-12px, small controls 8px. Round controls are reserved for compact actions and avatars.
- Navigation, notices, and dialogs occupy layers 10, 12, and 30.

## Interaction

The desktop room introduction stays sticky beside normal document scrolling. Mobile uses a single column. Room images move slightly on pointer hover; scroll reveals introduce rooms. Reduced-motion preference disables movement.

Conversation progress represents completed questions. Partner mood represents response-based pressure. They are separate displays. No timer is shown.

Voice selection, stop playback, recording, three attempts per question, and the existing AI flow remain available. Saved reports can be reopened from the panel.

Dialogs trap focus, close with Escape, and return focus to the trigger. Every main action has keyboard focus and pressed feedback. Form errors remain inline.
